## Demo storyboard: 14–18 seconds, understandable with sound off

1. **0–2 seconds:** show the original bottle flip or skateboard jump. Small overlay: ORIGINAL VIDEO.
2. **2–6 seconds:** freeze at the apex; show the camera arc and return. Overlay changes to ONE FRAME → AI CAMERA MOVE.
3. **6–8 seconds:** resume the original action and show the payoff. Overlay: ORIGINAL VIDEO RESUMES.
4. **8–12 seconds:** show the app for four seconds: source frame, selected Swing Back preset, then finished result. Compress waiting honestly with a visible cut; do not imply measured instant generation.
5. **12–16 seconds:** replay the complete effect without UI. Small final label: FREEZE · H3 MAX ON FAL.

The effect goes first. The interface proves how it was made. Keep captions readable on a phone. Use actual generated footage, never animate the illustrative cover and present it as a model result. Avoid cutting away before the original action resumes: that payoff explains the whole idea.
